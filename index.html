I'm Madison Martinez, but you can call me Maddie.

My passion for makeup began in the seventh grade, and at just 15 years old, I turned that passion into my own makeup artistry business. Since then, I’ve been dedicated to helping my clients feel confident, beautiful, and their absolute best.

I specialize in a wide variety of makeup styles, from soft, natural glam to bold, full glam. Every look is customized to enhance your unique features, ensuring you leave my chair feeling confident, comfortable, and camera-ready.

I can't wait to be a part of your special day and help bring your vision to life!
